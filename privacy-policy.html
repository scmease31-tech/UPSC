Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

UPSC Daily Edge ("the App") is developed and operated by JD Vantage. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name and email address for account creation and authentication.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the App, including quiz scores, study streaks, bookmarks, and progress data to provide personalized features.
  • Device Information: We may collect device identifiers for advertising purposes (via Google AdMob) and app performance monitoring.
  • Crash Reports: We collect crash logs and error reports to improve app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Track your study progress, streaks, and quiz performance
  • Personalize your learning experience
  • Send push notifications for daily reminders (with your permission)
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Analyze app usage to improve features and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Firebase Authentication: For user sign-in and account management. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Cloud Firestore: For storing user data and app content. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve personalized ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API: For AI-powered search functionality. Queries are processed by Google's servers. Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform). All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Google AdMob: Device identifiers for advertising purposes
  • Firebase/Google: Analytics and crash data for app improvement

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the App
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings
  • Disable push notifications through your device settings

7. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by:

  • Deleting your account from the App's Profile/Settings screen
  • Emailing us at alisjodhani7858@gmail.com

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above (UPSC aspirants). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
